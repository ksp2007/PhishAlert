1. Who we are

PhishAlert is a browser extension that checks emails in Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail and Proton Mail for signs of phishing. Publisher: [TODO: name]. Contact: [TODO: email address].

2. The short version

  • We run no servers that receive your email. PhishAlert has no account system and no analytics or advertising. The only server we operate is the optional canary service described in section 5, which never receives email content.
  • Email subject, body and thread text are never sent anywhere unless you turn on “Send email content to Groq” (Settings → AI & Privacy). It is off by default.
  • Automatic classification of every opened email is a second, separate switch, also off by default.
  • Email text is never written to disk. When AI is on it is kept in browser memory for at most 30 minutes.

3. AI features (Groq) — only if you opt in

If you switch on AI features and have added your own Groq API key, then when you use an AI tool (summary, tone check, “why dangerous”, draft reply, calendar/action/label extraction, coach, or chat) PhishAlert sends Groq Inc. the open email’s subject and body (first ~3,000 characters); for thread-aware replies, earlier messages in the thread (up to ~800 characters each); and, for chat/explanations, the scan result (sender domain, score, check results). The inbox digest and reports send scan-history entries (sender addresses, domains, threat labels). Requests go directly from your browser to api.groq.com using your key. Groq’s own terms and privacy policy govern what Groq does with that data; review them before enabling. If you turn on automatic classification, each email you open is sent without a click.

Turning the switch off stops all of the above immediately and deletes any email text held in memory.

Canary service (operated by the publisher, https://phishalert-canary.phishalert-canary.workers.dev) — only when you press “Deploy Bait”A randomly generated install key (stored on the server only as a hash), and for each canary you create: the sender’s domain, the scan identifier (the mail client’s message or thread ID from the page address, or the sender domain if none is available), a campaign identifier, the risk score and the bait type (pixel or link). No email subject, body or recipient data is sent. If a bait pixel or link is later opened by anyone, the server records that request’s IP address, network operator, country, User-Agent and timestamp — this describes the party that opened the bait, not you. Canaries and their hit records are deleted automatically after 30 days; an install key expires after about 13 months. Registration is rate-limited by IP address, and the IP is used only for that limit.

6. Your controls

  • Settings → AI & Privacy: turn AI features and automatic classification on or off.
  • Settings → wipe all data: removes everything the extension stores, including the key store.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data.
  • Requests about data held by third parties (Groq, VirusTotal, etc.) must go to those providers.
  • Canary data held on the publisher’s server expires automatically after 30 days; to have it removed sooner, use the contact address below.
