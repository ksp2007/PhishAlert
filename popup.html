Protection Toggles 4
Alert me when a suspicious or critical email arrives
Critical emails (score <30 or active threats) trigger persistent alerts. Suspicious emails (score <60) trigger standard alerts.
Warn me before sending to suspicious domains
Checks recipient domains against URLhaus malware feed, MX records, and IDN patterns before you send.
Check where a link actually leads the moment you click it, before your browser navigates
Goes beyond the passive link badges below: on every click, PhishAlert follows the real redirect chain and runs the final destination through URLhaus, Google Safe Browsing, IDN/brand-impersonation and domain-age checks — then shows an Allow/Warn/Block interstitial instead of letting the page load. Uses the same optional redirect-chain permission as "Link Redirect-Chain Scanning" below; without it, links are still checked by domain and threat feeds, just not followed hop-by-hop.
Check every page you open (address bar, bookmarks, any site) against phishing feeds, and block known phishing pages
Unlike Active Link Protection, this is not limited to links in email. The address of each page you open is sent to PhishTank and URLhaus (and Google Safe Browsing if you added a key). Turn this off if you do not want your browsing addresses checked this way.

Active Defense — Canary Intelligence
🟢 Ready — works automatically, nothing to set up
When you open a flagged email, go to Threats & Forensics → Active Defense and tap Deploy Bait. PhishAlert creates an invisible tracker and a decoy reply for you to review and send yourself. If the sender's infrastructure touches it, you'll see what kind of system it was.

Trace suspicious senders 🕵️
Canary Intelligence is optional and needs no setup. Here's how it works.
1 · Spot a flagged email
Open it, then go to Threats & Forensics → Active Defense.
2 · Tap “Deploy Bait”
PhishAlert creates an invisible tracker and a decoy reply. You review it and send it yourself — it is never sent automatically.
3 · See what touches it
If the sender’s system opens the tracker, you’ll see whether it looked like a bot or scanner, or a person — with country and network. It never names an individual.
🔒 Only a random tracker ID and the sender’s domain are used — never your email content, passwords or inbox. Tracker data deletes itself after 30 days, and nothing happens unless you tap Deploy Bait.
⚙️ One-time canary server setup — shown until it's done.
1. Double-click DEPLOY-CANARY.bat in the PhishAlert folder. It needs Node.js and a free Cloudflare account. When your browser opens, log in and click Allow.
2. When it finishes, it prints Done. Canary server: https://phishalert-canary.<something>.workers.dev. That means it filled in the URL for you.
3. Open chrome://extensions, click the reload icon on PhishAlert, then tap Deploy Bait.
